## 💼 Template 2: LinkedIn — In-Depth Professional Post

**Headline:** Building Autonomous Agentic Fraud Operations with TigerGraph, GraphRAG, and Groq LPUs 🛡️

Thrilled to share what we've engineered for the **TigerGraph × Hacker House Goa 2026 Challenge** with @TigerGraphDB and @247pmstudio: **FraudGuard AI**.

**The Challenge:**
Financial institutions process hundreds of thousands of fraud alerts daily. But an elevated risk score is merely an invitation to investigate. Analysts spend 30 to 90 minutes manually tracing accounts, checking device fingerprints, and drafting regulatory filings—often after the money has already left the bank.

**What We Built:**
FraudGuard AI is an autonomous, evidence-driven fraud investigation agent that takes an alert from initial trigger to defensible resolution in sub-second time.

**Key Architectural Innovations:**
🔹 **Bounded Graph Traversal on TigerGraph:** Zero look-ahead bias with point-in-time temporal queries inspecting multi-hop customer, card, and device neighborhoods.
🔹 **Information-Theoretic Active Learning:** Leverages Shannon Binary Entropy ($H(p)$) and Expected Value of Information (EVOI) to mathematically determine when cardholder outreach is justified vs when available evidence is conclusive.
🔹 **3D Pareto Counterfactual Decision Optimizer:** Balances Financial Loss Prevented, Customer Convenience (churn minimization), and FinCEN Regulatory Compliance.
🔹 **Groq LPU Acceleration:** Sub-second (~0.7s) synthesis of FinCEN Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) covering all 5 Ws & H using `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`.
🔹 **Continuous Exam Period Streaming (Innovation Track):** Scanned unflagged November–December transactions to detect emerging multi-account syndicates stored in graph memory.

**Results on Official 20 Exam Cases:**
- 16.57 seconds total execution time (0.83s avg/case)
- 12 Confirmed Fraud | 6 Cleared Legitimate | 2 Uncertain / Escalated
- 5 Statutory FinCEN SARs filed ($4,614.16 in illicit exposure identified)
